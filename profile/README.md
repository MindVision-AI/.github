# 🧠 MindVision Lab

### 👁️ Advancing visual intelligence through perception and reasoning

👋 Welcome to **MindVision Lab**, an AI research group at Wuhan University
led by [Dr. Chaofeng Chen](https://chaofengc.github.io/).

We study how machines can perceive and understand the visual world,
with the goal of building intelligent systems that reason about
and interact with their surroundings.

## 🔬 Research interests

- 🎨 **Visual generation** — creating visual content with AI
- 🎬 **Image and video quality assessment** — understanding and evaluating visual quality
- 🛰️ **AI for remote sensing** — applying intelligent methods to Earth observation

🌐 Visit our [lab website](https://chaofengc.github.io/group.html) to
learn more about our team and research.
